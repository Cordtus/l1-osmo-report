1 · Executive summary

The GenesisL1 native token (el1) trades on Osmosis exclusively against OSMO in GAMM pool 732, the only OSMO/L1 pair. Across the full history the pool has turned over $1,052,077 over 40,856 trades by 5,873 wallets. Activity is thin and episodic with a strong 2026 revival; current TVL is $15,906, L1 trades at $0.2279 (6.37 OSMO). All-time bridged value is $449,647 in / $590,295 out, and net LP value flow is $3,506.

USD across the whole range. Numia's pools_volume gives USD volume from the pool's first trade (2022-06-06). Dividing that per-minute USD by the swap token volume recovers USD prices, so USD prices, LP flows and bridge flows are valued for the entire trading history — not only from 2023-11-16 when liquidity snapshots begin. Where both exist the derived USD price matches Numia's snapshot price to within ~1% (median).

6 · Caveats

  • Swap/LP token_amount columns are FLOAT; at 18 decimals L1 amounts lose low-order precision. Use the ics20_* tables (BIGNUMERIC) for exact amounts.
  • USD prices before 2023-11-16 are derived (USD volume ÷ token volume) and are smoothed with a 7-day median; treat them as estimates. From 2023-11-16 the USD price comes directly from liquidity snapshots.
  • TVL/USD liquidity has no source before 2023-11-16 — the pool balances are not reconstructable from the available tables.
  • osmosis_lock_tokens and osmosis_superfluid_staking are broken upstream (backing lf-data tables 404) and are excluded.
  • Numia is migrating Osmosis data from BigQuery to ObsessionDB (ClickHouse); this is a point-in-time snapshot.
